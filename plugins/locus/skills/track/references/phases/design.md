# Design

## Preconditions

The human agrees the Feature is ready for a formal repository contract.

## Workflow

1. Move or rename the local draft into a specification.
2. Convert the linked Project draft when one exists; otherwise create the Issue
   directly in the owning repository.
3. Keep the initial Issue concise and apply only supported, unambiguous metadata.
4. Refine problem, outcome, decisions, constraints, interfaces, verification,
   and non-goals until the human approves the specification.
5. Publish the approved specification as a separate Issue comment without its
   local Tracking section.

## Outcome

Record approval and continue to Plan. Do not implement an unapproved design.
